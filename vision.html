WHERE I AM

The starting point, honestly

Two companies operating, one of them with paying customers and a live payment rail, 59 sites and apps built, and a school timetable to work around. Early stage, not paper stage.

The gap between here and Vision 2040 is not ambition. It is capital, team and time, in that order.

ROADMAP

1, 5, 10 and 20 years

1 year — 2027

Make both companies pay properly

Sageco Evergreen with verified inventory at scale and broker partnerships; DeryCode with recurring support contracts rather than one-off builds. Close the first outside investment round.

5 years — 2031

Build the operating machine

A team that runs delivery without him in every detail, a property platform that is the default verified route to land in western Uganda, and a portfolio of small-business software with recurring revenue.

10 years — 2036

From operator to allocator

Start investing in other founders while still operating: small cheques, real mentorship, and a track record of returns that is not a story about potential.

20 years — 2046

Global investor, African focus

Capital deployed across Africa and beyond, with a portfolio of companies solving the problems he grew up inside. The investor he needed when he started.

VISION 2030

The next checkpoint

By 2030 the goal is simple to state and hard to do: verification and affordable software become normal in western Uganda, because his companies made them normal.

  • A property platform where a first-time buyer can complete a verified purchase without a middleman holding their hand
  • Hundreds of small businesses running software they own, can afford and can operate offline
  • A team of Ugandan engineers and operators earning from work that did not exist here five years earlier
  • An investment record, not an investment ambition

VISION 2040

The long direction

My long-term ambition is to work as an investor on the world stage, backing the people who build. I want to earn my way to that table: build companies that actually make money and actually help people, learn how capital flows, then put that capital behind African founders solving African problems. Patient money, trusted partnerships, deals done on transparency.

I want to be the investor I needed when I was starting out. Vision 2040 is a direction, not a claim: a commitment to keep learning, keep building and keep lifting others as I grow.

FINANCIAL & WEALTH-BUILDING JOURNEY

How the money is meant to work

The plan is unglamorous: earn from software, reinvest into products that compound, avoid debt that assumes growth he cannot control, and keep the companies able to pay their own bills before chasing capital.

Three rules he works by: price work honestly, keep costs small enough to survive a bad quarter, and never let a client's project depend on money he does not have yet.

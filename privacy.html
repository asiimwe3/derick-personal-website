Privacy policy

What this site collects, what it does not, and how to have your data removed.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

What this site collects

This is a static website. It does not set advertising cookies, does not use third-party tracking pixels, and does not build a profile of you. There is no login and no account.

  • No server-side logs are controlled by this site. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs under its own policy.
  • Local visit counts. The site stores a simple page-visit count in your own browser's local storage. It never leaves your device and can be cleared by clearing site data.
  • Theme preference. Your light or dark choice is stored in your browser only.
  • Search and the assistant run entirely in your browser against a file on this site. Your questions are not sent anywhere.

When you contact me

If you email or message, I receive what you send: your name, address and message. I use it to reply and keep it only as long as the conversation or the working relationship requires. I do not sell or share contact data.

Newsletter

The newsletter form opens your own email app and sends a subscription request to me directly. No third-party mailing system receives your address until one is connected, and if that changes this page will say which provider is used.

Your rights

You can ask what personal data I hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask for it to be deleted. Email derickasiimwe849@gmail.com and it will be handled.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.

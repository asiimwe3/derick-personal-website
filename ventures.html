FOUNDER

DeryCode Technologies

DeryCode Technologies builds software for Ugandan businesses that has to survive real conditions: weak internet, small budgets, no IT department. The company writes websites, offline-first business systems, AI tools and digital campaigns, then keeps them running after launch.

Why it exists: most software sold in Uganda is priced and designed for a different economy. It assumes fast internet, card payments and someone on staff to administer it. DeryCode builds the opposite: light, resilient, affordable, supported.

What it delivers: business websites and booking systems, offline-first accounting and records, payment integration through PesaPal and mobile money, technical SEO, and AI assistants embedded in the product.

Who it serves: hotels and lodges, property companies, machinery and retail shops, clinics, schools, community organisations and event businesses.

CEO

Sageco Evergreen

Sageco Evergreen is a property platform built to remove the fear from buying land in Uganda. Instead of a listing and a phone number, every property carries verification, GPS boundaries, a digital land passport and a payment path protected by escrow.

The problem it attacks: families lose savings to land they cannot verify. Proof lives in drawers, boundaries live in memory, and the buyer carries all the risk. That is an information failure, and information failures can be engineered against.

What is live: 63 verified property listings, GPS-verified boundaries, escrow-protected milestones, paid viewings, fractional investment features, an Android app, and a PesaPal payment rail in production.

Where it is going: verified inventory at scale, broker partnerships, and land transactions that a first-time buyer can complete without a lawyer holding their hand.

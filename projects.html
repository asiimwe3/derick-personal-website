CASE STUDIES

Three, in detail

Case study · Property

Sageco Evergreen: making verification a product feature

Problem. Land buyers in Uganda carry all the risk: proof in drawers, boundaries in memory, payment before verification.

Build. A platform where each listing carries GPS-verified boundaries, a digital land passport, escrow milestones and a paid viewing path, with an AI broker answering buyer questions and an Android app for field use.

Result. Live with 63 listings, working payments, and a security model rebuilt so the public key can read and submit but never modify or delete records.

Open the platform ↗
Case study · Hospitality

Relie Motel: from a Google pin to a booking channel

Problem. No website, no rates published, bookings only by phone, and a 3.8-star rating contradicted by guests describing five-star service.

Build. A multilingual, multi-currency site with direct WhatsApp booking and zero commission, plus a delivery document analysing 289 reviews, a tour-operator outreach pack and a 339-contact licensed operator list.

Result. A direct booking channel that costs nothing per reservation, and a reputation plan aimed at 4.3 stars within two quarters.

Open the site ↗
Case study · Retail

Nuwe Rugaba Machinery: turning one page into seven doors

Problem. A single-page site with one keyword, a Maps listing whose hours read as opening at midnight, and a misspelled domain that dead-ended correct-spelling traffic.

Build. Six category pages (pumps, generators, construction, welding, farm machinery, spares) with product structured data, FAQ schema, WhatsApp quote flows, a category grid on the homepage, a rebuilt sitemap and clean URLs.

Result. Seven indexable pages instead of one, each targeting what buyers actually search.

AI

Voice-first programming

Voice IDE explores a simple question: if someone can describe software out loud, should they need a keyboard to build it? Useful for builders with limited typing speed or literacy in English syntax.

Offline-capable assistants

Can a business assistant stay useful when the connection drops? Experiments in local knowledge bases and graceful degradation.

Long bio (150 words)

Asiimwe Derick is a Ugandan social entrepreneur from Kyenjojo in the west of the country. He is the founder of DeryCode Technologies, a software company building offline-first business systems, websites and AI tools for businesses that work without reliable internet or large budgets, and the CEO of Sageco Evergreen, a property platform designed to remove the fear from buying land through verified listings, GPS-verified boundaries, escrow-protected payments and digital land passports. Born in January 2006, he runs both companies while completing school, and has delivered 59 live websites and applications across property, hospitality, education, retail, health and agriculture. His stated mission is to change lives at scale, and his long-term ambition, Vision 2040, is to move from building companies to investing in the founders building Africa's future.

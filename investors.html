THE OPPORTUNITY

Two companies, one thesis

DeryCode Technologies

Software for the businesses everyone else ignores

Offline-first systems and websites for Ugandan small businesses, sold at a price the market can pay and supported after launch. Recurring support contracts and products like DeryAccount sit on top of a delivery business that already has clients.

Sageco Evergreen

Trust as a product in a $ billions land market

Verified listings, GPS boundaries, escrow-protected payments, land passports and fractional investment. Live with 63 listings, a production payment rail and paid viewings already running.

The shared thesis: in markets with weak infrastructure, the winner is whoever makes the honest transaction the easy one. Verification for land, affordability for software. Both are unglamorous, defensible, and hard to copy without being on the ground.

WHAT I'M LOOKING FOR

The ask, stated plainly

What you get: direct access to the founder, published numbers, and a partner who has already built and shipped 59 live products.

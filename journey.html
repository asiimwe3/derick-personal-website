CAREER & PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

What I do professionally

Derick works as a founder and operator, not a consultant on the side. Professionally he runs two companies at once: one that builds software for other businesses, and one that uses software to fix a market he grew up watching fail.

Founder & CEO, DeryCode Technologies. Leads product, engineering and client delivery: websites, offline-first business systems, AI tooling and digital presence for Ugandan businesses. Responsible for scoping, pricing, building, deploying and supporting.

CEO, Sageco Evergreen. Leads a property platform: verified listings, GPS-verified boundaries, escrow-protected payments, digital land passports and fractional investment features. Responsible for product direction, partner relationships and operating discipline.

Working style. Small teams, direct communication, ship then improve, publish the numbers, and stay reachable after launch.
